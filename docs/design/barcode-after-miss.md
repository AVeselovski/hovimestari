## For the owner

**Build it. No new table, no migration. Your own food carries the barcode, and every path after a miss can reach one.**

**What you will be able to do.** Your olive oil, step by step, in a recipe:

1. In the recipe editor you press **Add ingredient**, then the camera, and scan the bottle. The block says "No food has the barcode 6416710030208." with **Search by name**, **Add it from the label** and **Scan again**.
2. You press **Search by name**. The field empties for you to type. A line now sits at the top of the list: "Barcode 6416710030208 goes on the food you save as your own.", with **Forget the barcode** under it.
3. You type "oliivi" and tap Fineli's "Oliiviöljy".
4. Under its amount you press **Copy to my foods with the barcode**. Every food from Fineli or Open Food Facts has **Copy to my foods**, after a miss or not. After a miss the button says it takes the barcode.
5. The food form opens with Fineli's name, its four values and the barcode filled in, and Fineli's portions (decilitre, tablespoon, teaspoon and the rest) listed at the end by name, in the order your food will list them. A line says "These values are Fineli's. Change any that differ from the label." You change fat to the label's figure and the name to "Oliiviöljy Filos", and press **Save food**.
6. You land on the amount of **your** olive oil, not Fineli's, with the grams you had typed still there, and press **Add**. The line in the recipe is your food.
7. Fineli's olive oil stays exactly as it was. Your next scan of that bottle opens your olive oil at once.

In the log sheet, step 4 is the same button in the food's view, just under "From Fineli · About the data" and above the figures per 100 g. On a phone it is in view as the food opens, or a short scroll away on a small one. Copy before you log, because a log ends the carry. The same steps end on your olive oil's own view, with any grams you typed still there, ready to log.

The two other paths after **Search by name**:

- **Nothing close in Fineli.** Your search shows "Add “Filos oliiviöljy” as your own food". The form opens with that name and the barcode filled in, for the label's values.
- **You already have the food, without the barcode** (log sheet). Open it, press **Edit food**, then **Use barcode 6416710030208** under the barcode field, and **Save food**.

Your own food now shows its barcode under its name, so you can see what a scan will find. To take a barcode off, edit the food, empty the barcode field and save.

**What stays a limit.**

- The barcode goes only on a food of your own. Picking Fineli's olive oil and logging it as it is leaves the barcode behind, and the next scan misses again.
- Your copy is a new food. It starts with no last amount, it is not in your recent items and it is not starred. It has one name, the one you see, so a search in the other language does not find it. Recipes, days and favourites that already use Fineli's olive oil keep using it, so the bottle becomes two foods across your recipes.
- Your copy keeps the values you saved. When Fineli corrects its olive oil in a later release, your copy does not follow.
- Both olive oils show in your search, yours marked as your own.
- A food has one barcode. Two pack sizes with two barcodes need two foods, or the second scan replaces the first barcode.
- A pack weighed in the shop and a number with a wrong digit carry no barcode, as today, because neither can find a food again.
- In the recipe's ingredient search you cannot put the barcode on a food of yours you already have. The log sheet can, through **Edit food**.
- An amount typed as a count of portions, such as 2 tablespoons, is not kept on your copy. An amount in grams is.
- The barcode is carried until you leave the sheet, log a food, add an ingredient, scan or type another barcode, save it onto a food, or press **Forget the barcode**.
- When your assistant copies a food for you, Activity shows the food it made, not the portions that came with it.

**Choices that are yours, each with what is built until you say otherwise.**

1. **A direct tie without a copy** (your option 3). Built: not built. A tie would keep one olive oil everywhere: your recipes, days, star, last amount and both its names stay as they are, it follows Fineli's corrections, and it is one press. A copy is two presses and a form to look over, it starts fresh with no last amount and no star, it has only the name you see, and recipes that already use Fineli's olive oil keep it. What the tie costs: a new table, a database change, a step in the lookup, a way to see and remove ties, two assistant tools, and a rule for a Fineli food a later release retires. The copy is what you said you prefer, and it needs none of that. If those copy costs bother you in practice, the tie can be added later beside it.
2. **A copy saved without changes reads as your own food.** Built: yes. Its source line says it is your food, though every figure in it is Fineli's. Please say yes to this explicitly. The other choice shows "Copied from Fineli" under its name, which needs a database change.
3. **Copy to my foods on every Fineli food, not only after a miss.** Built: on every Fineli and Open Food Facts food, because you asked to edit a Fineli entry into your own food in general. The other choice shows it only while a barcode is carried.
4. **Portions come with the copy.** Built: yes, because olive oil is logged by the tablespoon and the copy should not be worse than what you left. They become your own portions, which you can change. The other choice copies the name and the four values only.
5. **The star does not come with the copy.** Built: your copy starts unstarred. Star it if you want it in Favourites.
6. **The wording** of the carried line and the buttons, listed in section f.

Both requirement changes this needs are in `TODO.md` under the decisions that are yours.

## a) Overview

The finder (`useFoodFinder`) learns to carry a barcode: **Search by name** in the barcode block hands it the barcode that **Add it from the label** would have kept, and the finder holds it until the person logs a food, adds an ingredient, enters another barcode, saves it onto a food, presses **Forget the barcode**, or the sheet closes. While carried, a line on the search says so, every new own food the finder opens starts with it, and an edit of an own food offers it by a button. **Copy to my foods** moves into one shared component, shows on every shared food (Fineli and Open Food Facts), names the barcode when one is carried, and copies the food's portions. `POST /foods` and `create_own_food` take an optional list of portions, so a copy is one atomic create. The lookup is unchanged, no table is added, and there is no migration.

## c) Data model

**No migration.** Every part fits the existing tables and constraints:

- A copy is an ordinary `foods` row with `source = 'user'`, the owner's `user_id`, the name and the four values the person saved, and the barcode. `foods_user_barcode_uq` refuses a second own food with the same string, which the form shows as `barcode_taken` under the field.
- Its portions are `food_portions` rows with `user_id` set, `name` set and `fineli_unit` null: the shape `addPortion` writes today. `food_portions_one_label` and `food_portions_fineli_unowned` hold. Every Fineli portion's grams fit `numeric(6,1)` between 0 and 5000, as `PortionBodySchema` asks. The cascade walk is unchanged, because no new key and no new table appear.
- A Fineli unit portion is copied as a named portion whose name is the catalog's English label at the moment of copy ("Tablespoon"). From then on it is the person's data, renamed or deleted like any own portion.
- The shared food, its portions, entries, ingredient lines, favourites and `food_lookups` rows are not touched.

```ts
// packages/types/src/foods.ts
export const MAX_NEW_FOOD_PORTIONS = 30;

export const OwnFoodBodySchema = OwnFoodUpdateBodySchema.extend({
  name: /* unchanged */,
  barcode: /* unchanged */,
  // Create only: the portions a new food comes with, such as a copy's.
  portions: z
    .array(PortionBodySchema)
    .max(MAX_NEW_FOOD_PORTIONS, { error: "portions_too_many" })
    .optional(),
});

export type OwnFoodUpdateBody = z.infer<typeof OwnFoodUpdateBodySchema>;
```

`.optional()` and not `.default([])`, so `OwnFoodBody`'s output type does not require the field, and the web's `parseOwnFood`, which parses with the update schema, keeps its type.

## d) API interface

### `POST /api/v1/foods`

Body: `{ name, per100g, barcode?, portions? }`. `portions` is a list of `{ name, grams }` by `PortionBodySchema`, at most 30. Answers 201 with the `FoodDetail`, whose `portions` now hold the copies, each `isOwn: true`. New refusals: 400 `portions_too_many`, and a portion's own codes (`portion_name_required`, `portion_name_too_long`, `portion_grams_invalid`). The REST answer carries `{ message, code }` and no index, as for every body but a recipe's lines. The assistant's tool gives the path `portions.<i>.<field>`, from the MCP input check. The food and its portions are one transaction: a refused portion saves nothing.

### `PUT /api/v1/foods/:id`

Unchanged. It reads `OwnFoodUpdateBodySchema`, which has no `portions`, so an edit never touches portions. To take a barcode off, the client sends `barcode: null`, as the form does when the field is empty.

### `POST /api/v1/foods/lookup`

Unchanged. Confirmed against `findOwnFood` in `services/writes/barcode.ts`: own foods first, matched padded to 14 digits and by UPC-E form, newest edit first. A copy saved with the scanned barcode is found on the next scan with no call to Open Food Facts.

### Assistant tools

No new tool. The whole olive oil story is in an assistant's reach: `look_up_barcode`, `search_foods`, `get_food`, then `create_own_food` with the barcode and portions. An existing own food takes the barcode through `update_own_food`. The carried barcode is screen state: an assistant holds the barcode in its own conversation.

`create_own_food`:

- New parameter `portions`: an optional list of `{ name, grams }`, described as "Portions the food comes with, such as those of a food you copy. At most 30. A Fineli portion has a unit code and no name: give it a name the person reads, such as Tablespoon for RKL."
- New description: "Add one of the person's own foods, with the values per 100 g from its label, and optionally its barcode and portions. To make a food from Fineli or Open Food Facts the person's own with their changes, read it with get_food and pass its name, values and portions, changed as the person asks. The food copied stays as it is. A food with a barcode is what the person's next lookup of that barcode finds. It is then found by search_foods and can be logged with log_food. Answers the food with its id."
- The record of the change stays `food.created` with the food's id, name, values and barcode. Its portions are not recorded (decision 0.11). The answer holds the portions.

`update_own_food` is unchanged: a barcode set to null removes it, which undoes a tie.

## g) Constraints and decisions

- **An own food is the only thing a barcode is tied to.** It already wins every lookup and already has its edit, delete, tool and visibility rules. A second mechanism would duplicate those for one outcome.
- **One mount site per host for the copy, one component.** `CopyToOwnButton` replaces the two copies of the Open Food Facts button. A label that varies by `carried` is one control, not two.
- **The barcode travels in the finder, nowhere else.** Both hosts own a finder per open. A module global would outlive sign-out. A query cache entry would outlive the sheet.
- **Only choosing a food ends the carry.** A log, an add, another barcode, a save that uses it, or the person's own Forget. `reset` empties the field for other reasons, such as a delete, and leaves the carry alone.
- **Nothing attaches without Save on a form that shows the code.** Every path that writes the barcode ends at **Save food** with the barcode in its field.
- **A copy is one create.** Portions ride in the create body, so the food never exists without them, and an assistant's copy is the same single call.
- **The lookup does not change.** `findOwnFood` already answers the copy.
- Module rules: the web imports from `@hovimestari/types` without `.js`. The backend files keep `.js` relative imports.

## Resolved defaults (orchestrator's brief, 2026-11-26, owner may veto)

- A new food made while carried starts with it in the barcode field. An edit offers it by **Use barcode {code}** and never fills it unasked.
- A carried code wins over an Open Food Facts food's own barcode in a copy.
- An own food's source line shows its barcode.

- The picker gets no edit of an existing own food.
- `POST /foods` and `create_own_food` take up to 30 portions. `PUT` and `update_own_food` take none, typed so on the server and the web.
